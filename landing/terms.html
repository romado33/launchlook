The service

LaunchLook provides a pre-launch checkup of your publicly accessible web app (including vibe-coded apps and Webflow sites). We capture screenshots and HTML from your live URL, draft findings, and Rob (the founder) personally reviews and edits the list before delivery. You receive PDF report(s) by email. Paid deliveries also include a private shareable page at launchlook.app/r/{your-slug} (private until you opt in to share). We report issues visible to end users and supply paste-into-builder fix text. We do not guarantee your app is secure, bug-free, or ready for production.

Free audit

The free tier delivers up to 2 findings by email. No payment required. Same human review standards, but narrower scope than paid tiers. Free audits are rate-limited. No warranty beyond what is stated in these terms and our refund policy (refunds apply to paid purchases only).

Paid tiers and add-ons

Paid tiers (Starter $19, Scale Up $49, Pro $99) include more findings and bundled PDFs as described on the pricing page. Optional add-ons include:

  • Broken Flow Review ($49): post-launch only. One named failing flow, up to ~5 focused findings. Not a full pre-launch audit.
  • Fix Check: a re-scan after you apply fixes (priced at delivery; discounted within 14 days of the original audit).
  • Handoff Report: included on Pro; available as an add-on on lower tiers. Formatted for a developer you hire next.

Add-ons follow the same refund window unless we say otherwise in writing.

Not a security audit

Our checkup is not penetration testing or compliance certification. The Scale Up ($49) and Pro ($99) tiers include a basic cross-user visibility check, not a full security audit. The Pro Package integrations review covers configuration of Stripe, auth, email, and analytics services from the customer-facing surface only; it is not a security review of those providers. For security-focused reviews, use a dedicated security scanner or hire a security professional.
