Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

LaunchLook is operated by Rob Dods (hello@launchlook.app). We run pre-launch checkups for web apps. AI helps draft findings; I (Rob) edit everything before you get the report. This policy covers what we collect and how we use it. See also our Terms of Service.

What we collect

  • Free audit (homepage): your app URL, email, and optional platform (Lovable, Bolt, Webflow, etc.). We may store your IP address briefly to prevent abuse (rate limits).
  • Purchase info: name, email, and payment details (processed by Stripe; we do not store card numbers).
  • Intake form (Tally): your app URL, description, builder/platform (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor, Webflow, or similar), tier purchased, and optional temporary test account credentials for Scale Up ($49) and Pro ($99) audits.
  • Audit artifacts: screenshots and notes from reviewing your publicly reachable app. Your delivered report PDFs may include these screenshots.

What not to submit

Please use the intake form only (not email) for credentials. Do not send:

  • Your personal password or production admin credentials
  • API keys, Stripe keys, Supabase service keys, or database connection strings
  • Private customer data, health information, children's data, or financial records unless you have permission and understand the risks

For Scale Up ($49) and Pro ($99) user data isolation checks, create temporary test accounts only. Delete or rotate those credentials after we deliver your report.

Free audit

The free 2-finding audit uses the same review process as paid checkups, but we only email the top two findings. No payment required. We queue requests in our internal workspace (Notion), apply the same AI-assisted draft + human review, and send results by email. Free requests are limited per email and IP to prevent abuse.

Shareable report pages

Every paid delivery includes a private link at launchlook.app/r/{your-slug}. It is private by default. If you reply “share” to your delivery email, we flip it public so you can post the link. Public pages show sanitized findings (your live URL and email are stripped). Screenshots from the audit are not published on that page. You can reply “unshare” to make it private again.

How we use your information

We use your information only to deliver your checkup report, send transactional email, handle refunds, and improve our service. We do not sell your data.

AI processing

We use AI (Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI's GPT models) to analyze screenshots and HTML from your live URL. Your app data goes to those providers only to generate the draft audit. We do not use your data to train models. The providers do not keep screenshots or HTML past the audit run. I review and edit on our side before anything is emailed to you.

Test account credentials

If you provide test logins for a Scale Up ($49) or Pro ($99) audit, we use them only during the audit. We will not make purchases, change production data, delete records, invite users, send messages, or perform destructive actions unless you explicitly agree in writing beforehand. Credentials are deleted within 24 hours of report delivery.

Retention

Audit artifacts (screenshots and review notes) are kept for 30 days, then deleted.

Customer record means: your name, email, order date, tier purchased, app URL, delivery status, and support/refund history. We may keep this longer in our internal workspace (Notion) for support, tax, refund handling, and referral tracking.

Third-party services

  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Tally: post-purchase intake forms
  • Notion: internal operations and customer tracking (including free audit queue)
  • Resend: report delivery email (PDF attachments), free-audit confirmations, and other transactional email
  • Vercel: website and API hosting
  • Anthropic (Claude) and/or OpenAI (GPT): AI analysis of screenshots and HTML for the initial audit draft. Inputs are processed for the audit and not used for model training. See AI processing above.

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g. Plausible) on the marketing site. If enabled, it does not use advertising cookies and does not track you across other sites.

Your rights

Email hello@launchlook.app to request access, correction, or deletion of your data. We do not sell personal information. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children

LaunchLook is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.
